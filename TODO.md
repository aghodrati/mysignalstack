Preferred fix: option 1, just raise the three numeric limits (not per-source limits or smarter start+middle+end truncation). Deferred until current debugging/stabilization work wraps up.

- **Commodities (gold, silver, ...) in Loop A's tracked universe** — not a good fit yet with the current design. Two blockers: (1) the fundamentals/critic layer (`finance/fundamentals.py`, `finance/critic.py`) is built entirely around company financials (P/E, revenue growth, margins, analyst targets) that don't exist for a commodity ETF like GLD/SLV; (2) the only active news source (`config_loop_a.json`, SemiAnalysis) is semiconductor/AI-focused and won't generate commodity-relevant claims anyway. Doing this properly would need a macro-focused news source *and* a separate, non-equity fundamentals check for commodities -- a real design change, not a config edit. (Gold/silver are already available for pure price/momentum/correlation on the comparison tabs via `finance/universe.py`'s `METALS` category, unrelated to Loop A.)
